# History of India, Ancient Civilizations, Medieval and Modern Eras

## 1. Ancient India & The Indus Valley Civilization
- **Indus Valley / Harappan Civilization ($\sim 2600\text{ - }1900\text{ BCE}$)**:
  - Advanced urban planning: Grid layout, baked brick houses, sophisticated covered drainage networks, public granaries, and the Great Bath at Mohenjo-daro.
  - Major archaeological sites: Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Lothal (dockyard in Gujarat), Dholavira (water harvesting reservoirs), Kalibangan (ploughed fields), Rakhigarhi.
  - Economy and craft: Bronze metallurgy, steatite seals with unicorn/pashupati motifs, bead making, extensive maritime trade with Mesopotamia (Meluhha).
- **Vedic Period ($\sim 1500\text{ - }500\text{ BCE}$)**:
  - Early Vedic (Rigvedic) era centered around the Sapta Sindhu region; pastoral economy, tribal assemblies (Sabha, Samiti).
  - Later Vedic era: Migration towards the Gangetic plains; emergence of settled agriculture, Painted Grey Ware (PGW), and social stratification.

## 2. Mauryan Empire, Emperor Ashoka, and The Classical Age
- **Mahajanapadas & Mauryan Empire ($322\text{ - }185\text{ BCE}$)**:
  - Emergence of 16 Mahajanapadas; Magadha rose as the preeminent power.
  - **Chandragupta Maurya** founded the Mauryan Empire with guidance from **Chanakya (Kautilya)**, author of the political treatise *Arthashastra*.
  - **Emperor Ashoka**: Following the Kalinga War ($261\text{ BCE}$), adopted Buddhism and formulated the policy of *Dhamma*, inscribed across Rock and Pillar Edicts using Brahmi, Kharosthi, and Greek scripts.
- **The Gupta Classical Golden Age ($\sim 320\text{ - }550\text{ CE}$)**:
  - Reign of Chandragupta I, Samudragupta, and Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya).
  - Flourishing of mathematics and astronomy: **Aryabhata** (place-value zero, solar system rotation) and **Varahamihira**. Literature: **Kalidasa** (*Shakuntala*, *Meghaduta*).

## 3. Medieval India: Empires and Culture
- **South Indian Kingdoms**:
  - **Chola Dynasty**: Imperial power under Rajaraja I and Rajendra I. Notable for autonomous village administration (Uttaramerur inscription), grand Dravidian temple architecture (Brihadeeswarar Temple, Thanjavur), and dominant naval expeditions across Southeast Asia (Srivijaya).
  - **Vijayanagara Empire ($1336\text{ - }1646\text{ CE}$)**: Founded by Harihara and Bukka with capital at Hampi; reached its zenith under **Krishnadevaraya**.
- **Delhi Sultanate ($1206\text{ - }1526\text{ CE}$)**: Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, and Lodi dynasties.
- **Mughal Empire ($1526\text{ - }1707\text{ CE}$)**:
  - Founded by Babur (First Battle of Panipat, 1526). Expanded under Akbar (administrative Mansabdari system, policy of religious harmony *Sulh-i-kul*).
  - Architectural peaks under Shah Jahan (Taj Mahal, Red Fort).
- **Maratha Empire**:
  - Founded by **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj** in the 17th century; pioneered innovative guerrilla warfare (*Ganimi Kava*), naval defense, and the *Ashta Pradhan* council of ministers. Expanded across large parts of the Indian subcontinent under the Peshwas.

## 4. Modern India and The Freedom Struggle
- **British Colonial Expansion**:
  - British East India Company gained revenue rights over Bengal following the Battle of Plassey (1757) and Battle of Buxar (1764).
  - **Revolt of 1857 (First War of Independence)**: Led by Mangal Pandey, Rani Lakshmibai, Bahadur Shah Zafar; led to Government of India Act 1858 transferring rule directly to the British Crown.
- **Indian National Movement**:
  - Formation of the Indian National Congress (1885).
  - Moderate and Extremist phases: Bal Gangadhar Tilak (*"Swaraj is my birthright and I shall have it"*), Bipin Chandra Pal, Lala Lajpat Rai.
  - **Mahatma Gandhi's Mass Movements**: Non-Cooperation Movement (1920), Civil Disobedience Movement / Dandi Salt March (1930), Quit India Movement (1942) grounded in *Satyagraha* and *Ahimsa*.
  - Revolutionary struggle: Bhagat Singh, Chandrashekhar Azad, Subhas Chandra Bose and the Indian National Army (INA).
  - **Independence & Constitution (1947–1950)**: Partition and independence on August 15, 1947. Adoption of the Constitution of India drafted under Dr. B.R. Ambedkar on January 26, 1950.
