# India: Government and Contemporary History

## Current Prime Minister

As of 2 October 2026, Narendra Modi is the Prime Minister of India. The Prime Minister's Office states that he was sworn in for a third term on 9 June 2024, after serving from 2014 to 2019 and from 2019 to 2024.

## India's Republic

India is a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic with a parliamentary system of government. The Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on 26 November 1949, and it came into force on 26 January 1950. The Prime Minister heads the Council of Ministers, which aids and advises the President; the Council of Ministers holds the Union's real executive power.
