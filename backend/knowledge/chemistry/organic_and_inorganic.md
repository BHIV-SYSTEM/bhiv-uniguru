# Organic Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry, and Periodic Table Trends

## 1. Periodic Table Periodic Trends
Periodic trends describe predictable patterns in chemical properties across periods (rows) and down groups (columns):
- **Atomic Radius**: Decreases across a period (due to increasing effective nuclear charge $Z_{\text{eff}}$ pulling electrons closer) and increases down a group (due to additional electron shells).
- **Ionization Energy**: The energy required to remove the outermost valence electron from an isolated gaseous atom:
  $$X(g) \to X^+(g) + e^-$$
  Increases across a period, decreases down a group.
- **Electronegativity**: Tendency of an atom to attract shared electron pairs in a chemical bond (Pauling Scale):
  Fluorine (F = 3.98) is the most electronegative element, followed by Oxygen (O = 3.44), Chlorine (Cl = 3.16), and Nitrogen (N = 3.04).
- **Valence Electrons & Octet Rule**: Main group elements react to achieve a stable noble gas electron configuration (8 valence electrons $ns^2 np^6$).

## 2. Chemical Bonding and Molecular Geometry
- **Ionic Bonding**: Complete electrostatic transfer of electrons between elements with large electronegativity difference ($\Delta EN > 1.7$, e.g., $\text{NaCl}$).
- **Covalent Bonding**: Mutual sharing of electron pairs ($\Delta EN < 1.7$):
  - Non-polar covalent ($\Delta EN < 0.4$, e.g., $H_2$, $CH_4$).
  - Polar covalent ($0.4 \le \Delta EN \le 1.7$, e.g., $H_2O$, $HCl$).
- **VSEPR Theory (Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion)**:
  Predicts 3D molecular geometry to minimize electron pair repulsion:
  - Linear: 2 electron domains ($180^\circ$, e.g., $CO_2$).
  - Trigonal Planar: 3 electron domains ($120^\circ$, e.g., $BF_3$).
  - Tetrahedral: 4 electron domains ($109.5^\circ$, e.g., $CH_4$).
  - Bent / Angular: 4 domains with 2 lone pairs ($104.5^\circ$, e.g., $H_2O$).

## 3. Organic Chemistry Mechanisms & Functional Groups
- **Functional Groups**:
  - Alkanes: Single bonds ($C_n H_{2n+2}$), combustion and free radical halogenation.
  - Alkenes: Double bonds ($C_n H_{2n}$), electrophilic addition reactions (Markovnikov's rule).
  - Alcohols: $-OH$ hydroxyl group.
  - Aldehydes & Ketones: Carbonyl group ($C=O$), nucleophilic addition.
  - Carboxylic Acids: $-COOH$, acidic proton, esterification.
- **Nucleophilic Substitution Mechanisms**:
  - **$S_N1$ / SN1 (Substitution Nucleophilic Unimolecular)**: Two-step mechanism via a carbocation intermediate; favored by tertiary substrates and polar protic solvents; results in racemization.
  - **$S_N2$ / SN2 (Substitution Nucleophilic Bimolecular)**: Single-step concerted mechanism with backside attack; favored by primary substrates; results in complete Walden inversion of stereocenters.
