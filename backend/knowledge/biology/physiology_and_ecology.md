# Plant Physiology, Photosynthesis, Human Systems, and Ecology

## 1. Photosynthesis (Light & Dark Reactions)
Photosynthesis converts solar light energy into chemical potential energy in autotrophs:
$$6CO_2 + 6H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{Light, Chlorophyll}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2$$

- **Light-Dependent Reactions (in Thylakoid Membranes)**:
  - Photolysis of water: $2H_2O \to 4H^+ + 4e^- + O_2$.
  - Electron transport across Photosystem II ($P_{680}$) and Photosystem I ($P_{700}$) generates ATP via photophosphorylation and reduces $NADP^+$ to $NADPH$.
- **Light-Independent Reactions (Calvin Cycle in Stroma)**:
  - **Carbon Fixation**: Enzyme **RuBisCO** catalyzes addition of $CO_2$ to Ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP) forming 3-phosphoglycerate (3-PGA).
  - **Reduction**: 3-PGA is reduced to Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate (G3P) using ATP and NADPH.
  - **Regeneration**: RuBP is regenerated to sustain the cyclic pathway.

## 2. Human Organ Systems
- **Circulatory System**: Closed double circulation (systemic and pulmonary circuits). The 4-chambered heart pumps deoxygenated blood to the lungs via the pulmonary artery and oxygenated blood to the body via the aorta.
- **Nervous System**: Neurons transmit electrical action potentials ($\sim -70\text{ mV}$ resting potential to $+30\text{ mV}$ peak depolarization mediated by voltage-gated $Na^+$ and $K^+$ channels) across chemical synapses using neurotransmitters (acetylcholine, dopamine).
- **Endocrine System**: Ductless glands release hormones directly into bloodstream (e.g., Insulin and Glucagon from pancreatic Islets of Langerhans regulating blood glucose homeostasis).

## 3. Ecology, Biogeochemical Cycles, and Evolution
- **Trophic Levels & 10% Energy Rule (Lindeman's Law)**: Only approximately 10% of energy transferred between trophic levels is assimilated into biomass; the remaining 90% is dissipated as metabolic heat.
- **Biogeochemical Cycles**:
  - **Hydrological (Water) Cycle**: Evaporation, Transpiration, Condensation, Precipitation, Infiltration, Surface Runoff.
  - **Carbon Cycle**: Photosynthesis fixes atmospheric $CO_2$; respiration, decomposition, and combustion return $CO_2$ to the atmosphere.
  - **Nitrogen Cycle**: Atmospheric $N_2$ fixed into ammonia ($NH_3$) by nitrogen-fixing bacteria (Rhizobium, Azotobacter), followed by nitrification into nitrites ($NO_2^-$) and nitrates ($NO_3^-$), and denitrification back to $N_2$.
- **Darwinian Evolution by Natural Selection**:
  1. Overproduction of offspring with heritable genetic variations.
  2. Competition for limited environmental resources (struggle for existence).
  3. Differential survival and reproductive success of organisms with advantageous adaptations.
