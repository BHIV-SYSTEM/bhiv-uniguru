# Cell Biology, Molecular Genetics, and Biotechnology

## 1. Cell Structure and Organelles
The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms (Cell Theory by Schleiden, Schwann, and Virchow).
- **Prokaryotes vs. Eukaryotes**:
  - Prokaryotes (Bacteria, Archaea): Lack a membrane-bound nucleus and membrane-bound organelles; have circular 70S ribosomes.
  - Eukaryotes (Plants, Animals, Fungi): Possess a distinct nucleus, 80S ribosomes, and specialized organelles.
- **Key Organelles**:
  - **Mitochondria**: Site of aerobic cellular respiration, ATP production (Krebs cycle and electron transport chain); contains own circular DNA.
  - **Chloroplasts**: Site of photosynthesis in plants; contain thylakoid membranes with chlorophyll pigments.
  - **Ribosomes**: Non-membrane bound complexes of rRNA and proteins that carry out translation (protein synthesis).
  - **Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)**: Rough ER (studded with ribosomes) synthesizes secretory proteins; Smooth ER synthesizes lipids and detoxifies chemicals.
  - **Golgi Apparatus**: Modifies, packages, and sorts macromolecules for secretion or intracellular delivery.

## 2. Molecular Genetics & Central Dogma
The **Central Dogma of Molecular Biology** (proposed by Francis Crick) describes the unidirectional flow of genetic information:
$$\text{DNA} \xrightarrow{\text{Transcription}} \text{mRNA} \xrightarrow{\text{Translation}} \text{Protein}$$

- **DNA Replication**: Semi-conservative replication (Meselson-Stahl experiment). DNA Helicase unwinds the double helix, and DNA Polymerase III synthesizes new strands in the $5' \to 3'$ direction.
- **Transcription**: RNA Polymerase binds to gene promoter regions and synthesizes complementary pre-mRNA from the template DNA strand.
- **Translation**: Ribosomes decode mRNA codons (triplets of nucleotides) with complementary tRNA anticodons, sequentially assembling polypeptide chains. Start codon: $AUG$ (Methionine); Stop codons: $UAA, UAG, UGA$.

## 3. Mendelian Genetics and Biotechnology
- **Mendel's Laws of Inheritance**:
  1. **Law of Segregation**: Allele pairs separate during gamete formation so that each gamete carries only one allele for each gene.
  2. **Law of Independent Assortment**: Genes for different traits sort independently during gamete formation (valid for unlinked genes).
- **Recombinant DNA Technology**:
  - **Restriction Endonucleases**: Molecular scissors that cleave DNA at palindromic recognition sequences (e.g., EcoRI).
  - **PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)**: Exponentially amplifies targeted DNA segments across cycles of Denaturation ($94^\circ\text{C}$), Annealing ($55^\circ\text{C}$), and Extension ($72^\circ\text{C}$) via Taq polymerase.
