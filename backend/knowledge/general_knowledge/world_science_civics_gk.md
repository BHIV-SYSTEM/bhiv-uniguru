# General Knowledge, Indian Civics, World Capitals, and Scientific Milestones

## 1. Indian Constitution & Civics
The Constitution of India is the supreme law of India, adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into effect on 26 January 1950:
- **Preamble**: Declares India to be a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic securing Justice, Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity.
- **Fundamental Rights (Part III, Articles 12–35)**:
  1. Right to Equality (Articles 14–18).
  2. Right to Freedom (Articles 19–22, including speech and expression).
  3. Right against Exploitation (Articles 23–24).
  4. Right to Freedom of Religion (Articles 25–28).
  5. Cultural and Educational Rights (Articles 29–30).
  6. Right to Constitutional Remedies (Article 32, termed the *"Heart and Soul of the Constitution"* by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar).
- **Three Organs of State**:
  - **Legislature**: Parliament of India (President, Rajya Sabha / Council of States, Lok Sabha / House of the People).
  - **Executive**: President (Head of State), Prime Minister and Council of Ministers (Head of Government).
  - **Judiciary**: Integrated independent judiciary headed by the Supreme Court of India.

## 2. World Capitals and Major Global Bodies
- **Key National Capitals**:
  - India: New Delhi
  - France: Paris
  - United Kingdom: London
  - United States: Washington, D.C.
  - Japan: Tokyo
  - Germany: Berlin
  - Australia: Canberra
  - Brazil: Brasília
  - Russia: Moscow
  - China: Beijing
  - Egypt: Cairo
  - South Africa: Pretoria (administrative), Cape Town (legislative), Bloemfontein (judicial)
- **United Nations (UN)**:
  Founded on 24 October 1945 in San Francisco; headquarters in New York City. Principal organs: General Assembly, Security Council (15 members: 5 permanent P5 with veto power - US, UK, France, Russia, China, and 10 non-permanent), International Court of Justice (The Hague).

## 3. Landmark Scientific Discoveries
- **Periodic Table**: Formulated by Dmitri Mendeleev in 1869 based on atomic masses, modernized by Henry Moseley by atomic numbers ($Z$).
- **Structure of DNA**: Discovered by James Watson, Francis Crick, and Rosalind Franklin in 1953.
- **Penicillin**: First widely effective antibiotic discovered by Alexander Fleming in 1928.
- **Raman Effect**: Inelastic scattering of light photons discovered by Sir C.V. Raman in 1928 (National Science Day, Feb 28).
